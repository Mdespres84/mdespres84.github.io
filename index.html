Every company is different, so I start with the diagnosis, not the answer. Then one priority at a time, in order: data, then process, then technology, then scale.

Days 31–60 · Design
Fix what matters most

Design the fix with the team, not around it: structure, process, or tooling, whatever the diagnosis calls for.

Days 61–90 · Deliver
